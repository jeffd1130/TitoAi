## Caption

I plan a full week of content with one prompt.

Not a template. One message to Claude that says:
→ who the posts are for
→ how many posts and which days
→ what each post needs: hook, tool, prompt, call to action
→ "put it in a table"

Back comes a working plan in a table. I asked it for a sample carousel too.

What it doesn't do: decide for me. I still pick, cut and fix. But I never start from a blank page anymore.

The same structure works for a team lead's weekly priorities, a lesson plan or a product promo calendar: audience + quantity + required parts + format.
