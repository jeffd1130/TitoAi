# EP1 · Resume sa 5 Minuto — script + what to record

## What Jeff records (about 5 minutes)
1. Open **ChatGPT (free account, logged in to the free plan)** on your phone or laptop, logged in to the **free** plan (so "libre" stays true). New chat; hide your name/email if it shows.
2. Start the screen recording. Paste the sample notes, then the prompt, send, let it finish, scroll slowly through the resume. Whole run under 5 minutes (the voice says "limang minuto").
3. Send the file here or on Telegram. Don't edit the AI's answer; if it's bad, run it again and send the better one.

**Sample person (fictional, say "sample"): Ana Reyes, 24. 2 years cashier at a grocery (handled cash, closed the register, trained 2 new staff), 1 year online seller (Shopee, answered customer chats). Wants: customer service representative.**

**Prompt to paste:**
```
Gawin mo itong resume para sa Customer Service Representative. Ito ang mga ginawa ko: [i-paste ang sample notes sa itaas]. Gamitin lang ang binigay ko, huwag mag-imbento. Simple at malinaw na format.
```

## Voice (done): Gemini TTS, voice Finn → `raw/voice-finn.wav` (word timings `raw/voice-finn-words.json`)
