## Scenario (fictional, say "sample" on screen)
**Who you are:** chat support agent for "Tindahan ni Aling Rosa", a small online home-goods shop (made up).
**The customer:** Carla M. (made up). Ordered an electric fan on Nov 10, cancelled it the same day, was told the refund takes 5–7 business days. It's now 14 days, and she has followed up 3 times. Her chat today:

**Customer message (paste this):**
"Dalawang linggo na akong naghihintay ng refund ko! Tatlong beses na akong nag-follow up, wala pa rin. Order #10482 pa 'yan. Ang bagal ninyo! Kung wala pa bukas, ire-report ko na kayo."

**What the agent knows (paste right after the message):**
"Ang alam ko: na-approve na ang refund noong Nov 20. Nasa finance na, 3–5 banking days bago pumasok sa GCash niya. Hindi ko kayang pabilisin. Pwede kong i-escalate at bigyan siya ng update bukas ng 5 PM."

**Prompt (paste last, then send):**
```
Ito ang mensahe ng customer at ang alam ko. Gumawa ng kalmadong sagot: mag-sorry, ipaliwanag ang susunod na hakbang, huwag mangako ng hindi kaya. Maikli lang, Taglish, magalang.
```

**What a good answer should show (so the voice lines stay true):** a real sorry, the refund status (approved Nov 20, 3–5 banking days), the next step (escalate + update tomorrow 5 PM), and no promise of "today" or a date the agent can't control. If ChatGPT promises something you didn't give it, run it again and send the better one.
