## Scene Notes

**Scene 1 — Hook**
- Cold open — no music, no intro
- Hold 1 second of silence before the line
- Camera: tight on eyes

**Scene 2 — Tension**
- Mirror the audience's fear — say it out loud for them
- Delivery: slower than usual, pause after *"'Tito..."*

**Scene 3 — Reframe (MOST IMPORTANT)**
- This is the money moment — slow down here
- Emphasize: *"Pinalalayas ka ng taong gumagamit ng AI"*
- Pause after that line. Let it land.

**Scene 4 — Proof**
- Optional: 2-second screen cut to claude.ai or gemini.google.com homepage (no audio)
- Then cut back to face cam
- Delivery: matter-of-fact, not excited

**Scene 5 — Closer**
- Full warm energy returns
- Look directly at camera for the CTA
- End with Tito AI sign-off at natural pace
