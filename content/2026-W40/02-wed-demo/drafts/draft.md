## Production method and evidence

On Sep 30, 2026, the same safe prompt was submitted to fresh chats in ChatGPT (browser guest session), Gemini (Jeff's signed-in browser session), and Claude (Jeff's signed-in session, Haiku 4.5). No student data, client data, or private documents were used.

> Create a 20-minute beginner learning session for an adult Filipino practicing spoken English tonight. They have only 20 minutes and feel shy speaking aloud. Include a 2-minute warm-up, one short role-play, and two self-check questions. Keep it concise in Taglish.

Observed, not fabricated:

- **ChatGPT:** 0–2 warm-up, 2–8 easy speaking, 8–15 café role-play, 15–18 free speaking, 18–20 two-question self-check. Total labeled time: 20 minutes.
- **Gemini:** 2-minute warm-up, 15-minute café role-play with detailed dialogue, 3-minute two-question self-check. Total labeled time: 20 minutes.
- **Claude:** 2-minute warm-up, 14-minute focus (5 phrases + 2 pronunciation + 7 café role-play), 2-minute self-check, 1-minute closing. Total labeled time: 19 minutes, despite a "20-Minute" heading.

This is one run, not a repeatability study or a product ranking. The word "free" in project positioning should not be taken to mean the exact account/model tiers used here are guaranteed free to all viewers; do not claim that in the post. Generated human photos are illustrative and do not depict Jeff or an actual learner.
