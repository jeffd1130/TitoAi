# W25 Friday — Brief
**Slot:** 03-fri-inspiration
**Topic:** Nanay + AI — Ganito Namin Ginagamit sa Bahay
**Format:** Story / Inspiration Reel · 60–90s · Face cam talking head
**Drop:** Friday June 20, 2026 · 7:00 PM PHT

## Concept
Tito AI tells the story of a nanay (mom) who was scared of AI — and how she started using Claude at home, for real everyday tasks. Emotional, warm, relatable. Targets the nanay/parent audience who fears AI will replace them or their children.

## Hook
Do NOT open with "Kumusta" — lead with the story or the fear.
"May kakilala akong nanay — takot sa AI. Ngayon, siya na ang pinakamabilis sa grupo nila."

## Story arc
1. Fear hook — nanay scared, "baka palitan ako ng AI"
2. Her world — luto, laba, trabaho, budget ng pamilya
3. The moment — she tried Claude for one task (menu planning for the week)
4. The win — saved 2 hours, stayed within budget, kids loved the meals
5. Apply it yourself — "You can do this. Libre. 2 minuto lang."
6. CTA — Follow + closer

## Tone
Warm. Like telling a story to a pamangkin. Not technical. Not selling.
