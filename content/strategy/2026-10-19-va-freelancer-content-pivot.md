**Reviewed:** Oct 19, 2026 · **Status:** content direction for Jeff's approval, not a posting or revenue claim.

## Decision

Keep **“AI sa Totoong Buhay” for regular Pinoys**. Add a recurring **practice-to-portfolio** pathway for people curious about VA work, freelancing, or content creation. Do not turn every post into job advice, promise income, or imply a fictional practice brief is real client work.

**Audience ladder:** (1) everyday task anyone recognizes → (2) a free AI prompt → (3) visible output and its mistakes → (4) a human-corrected sample that could become a portfolio exercise. Use Taglish and one action per post. Keep Jeff as the host; the tool is a helper, not the hero.

## Creative tests

1. Open with a concrete task or AI slip, not “Kumusta.” Example: **“Isang fact lang binigay ko. Bakit may ‘paborito ng lahat’ si AI?”**
2. One visible before/after. The error must be a real observed output when presented as proof.
3. A repeatable comment CTA: **BRIEF** for the next fictional practice scenario. Never imply commenters will receive a job or paid client.
4. No paid boost until a new post earns organic signal. Compare saves, shares, comments, completion/retention if available, and follows; do not infer these from views alone.
5. Treat VA/freelance as one doorway into AI literacy; alternate with home, school, and small-business examples so regular Pinoys remain welcome.
