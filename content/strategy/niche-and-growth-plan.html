Niche & Target Audience

This is not a tech channel. It is for: freelancers, guro, BPO workers afraid of being replaced, nanays/tatays, small business owners — everyday Filipinos who need AI but don't know where to start.

Fear + aspiration = share-worthy content. Every video either removes a fear ("hindi mahirap 'to") or delivers a visible win ("ganito ang resulta").

Tito AI's Moat

  • Taglish — barrier to entry for foreign creators
  • "Libre lahat" — removes the #1 objection (cost)
  • Warm tito brand energy — no one else owns this positioning in PH
  • First mover — algorithm surfaces Tito AI when Filipinos search "AI tutorial Tagalog"

Pillar breakdown

  • Pillar 1 — Tool Demo (Mon / Wed): Highest save rate. Core educational content. "Paano gamitin ang Claude para sa negosyo mo — libre"
  • Pillar 2 — Before / After (Sat): Emotional, shareable, high retention. "3 oras ng trabaho → 5 minuto gamit ang AI"
  • Pillar 3 — Fear → Reassurance (evergreen): High anxiety = high engagement. "Hindi ka papalitan ng AI. Papalitan ka ng taong gumagamit ng AI."
  • Pillar 4 — Everyday Filipino Life (Fri): Warmth + identity. Builds community. Guro, nanay, negosyante stories.

Hook Formula

Every video must open in second 1 with one of:

  1. Fear hook — "Kung BPO worker ka, pakinggan mo 'to."
  2. Result hook — "30 seconds lang. Tingnan mo 'to." (show immediately)
  3. Identity hook — "May negosyo ka at wala kang marketing budget?"
  4. Curiosity hook — "Ito ang tanong na hindi mo pa naitatanong sa AI."

Never open with "Kumusta" first — hook lands in second 1, opener comes after.

Boost Strategy

Rule: Only boost posts that already have strong organic saves or shares — proven signal = lower CPM.

Process: Post organically → wait 24–48 hours → if 50+ saves OR 200+ shares → boost it.

Engagement Tactics

  1. End every video with a YES/NO or CHOICE question — "Claude o Gemini — alin ang subukan mo muna?"
  2. Reply to every comment in the first hour — TikTok/IG reward fast early engagement
  3. Pin first comment within 5 min — bonus tip or tool link
  4. Post times (PHT): TikTok = 8–10 PM · IG = 7–9 PM
  5. Share to Facebook Stories immediately after posting Reels
