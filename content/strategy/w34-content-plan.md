# Tito AI — W34 Content Plan
**Week:** Aug 17–23, 2026 · **Theme:** Mas Kaunting Rework, Mas Malinaw na Trabaho

## Objective

Increase useful engagement—not just views—by testing proof-first hooks, visible before/after outputs, save CTAs, and one binary question. Keep all posts organic unless 24-hour performance clearly exceeds the current 250–350-view baseline or generates qualified training inquiries.

## Audience and message

- **Primary:** Filipino freelancers, guro, BPO workers, and small business owners handling repetitive writing or revisions.
- **Core message:** Hindi AI ang bida—ang mas malinaw at mas mabilis mong trabaho ang bida.
- **Proof rule:** Every educational post must show a real, anonymized output before explaining the prompt.
- **Safety rule:** Use fictional/anonymized inputs only; remind viewers to follow workplace data policies.

## Post briefs

### Monday — Freelancer revision reply

- **S1:** “Tatlong revision. Isang malinaw na reply.”
- **S2:** Show the confusing fictional client feedback.
- **S3:** Show the cleaned summary and action list first.
- **S4:** Reveal the anonymized prompt.
- **S5:** “I-save mo ito bago ang susunod na revision.”
- **Prompt purpose:** Turn scattered feedback into: requested changes, questions to clarify, and a professional reply.

### Wednesday — Guro parent update

- Open on the finished parent update, then show the fictional student inputs.
- Demonstrate the output on screen; no text overlays on the video.
- Include: “Huwag maglagay ng pangalan o personal student data. Gumamit ng initials o fictional example.”
- End with one choice: **“Parent update o lesson plan—alin ang susunod?”**

### Friday — Training proof follow-up

- Use real CJEF room, laptop, teaching, and certificate footage.
- Focus on observable facts, not unverified participant quotations.
- Story arc: first training → what participants practiced → what Jeff learned → invitation.
- CTA: **“Comment GUSTO.”** Handle company/individual qualification in replies or DMs.
