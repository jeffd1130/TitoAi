# Tito AI Recalibration — Entertainment, Education, and Services

**Date:** September 22, 2026  
**Status:** Recommended direction for rollout  
**Working brand line:** **Tito AI — AI sa Totoong Buhay**

## Executive decision

Keep the **Tito AI** name, handle, navy/gold identity, and Jeff as the human host. Reposition the channel from a tutorial archive into an entertainment-led Filipino AI show with real experiments, real client systems, real seminar proof, and useful takeaways.

Do **not** use a full-time virtual avatar as the main presenter. Jeff's face, voice, work history, and teaching footage are the trust advantage that can convert viewers into seminar attendees and service clients. A disclosed animated **Tito Bot** sidekick can be tested for punchlines, myth alerts, scorecards, and transitions, but it should never replace Jeff or pretend to be human.

No plan can guarantee virality. This plan increases the odds through stronger packaging, faster feedback loops, recognizable shows, and conversion tracking.

## New positioning

> **For everyday Filipinos who want to grow with AI, Tito AI is the entertaining Taglish show that tests AI in real life—at work, at home, in school, and in business—because Jeff actually uses these systems with clients and teaches them in live seminars.**

### Promise

**Tawa muna. Subok natin. May matututunan ka.**

### Audience transformation

- Before: “Nakakatakot o komplikado ang AI.”
- After: “Nakita ko kung paano. Kaya kong subukan ngayon.”

### Brand personality

- Warm Tito, not tech guru
- Curious tester, not know-it-all
- Funny through situations and reactions, not forced jokes
- Evidence-led, not hype-led

## Recurring shows

### 1. Tito AI Tries

Jeff attempts a real task with a timer, constraint, or funny consequence.

- “Kaya bang ayusin ng AI ang isang linggong magulong notes?”
- “ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude: sino ang pinakamahusay na tutor?”
- “AI ang nagplano ng baon ko—pero papasa ba kay Tito?”

**Growth job:** shares and follows.

### 2. AI o Ay?

Fast reaction format. Show an output and let Jeff judge it: useful, unsafe, generic, or surprisingly good.

**Growth job:** comments and retention.  
**CTA:** “AI o Ay?”

### 3. Client Confidential

Anonymized before/after stories from real work. The client is the hero; Tito AI explains the system.

**Growth job:** qualified service inquiries.  
**Rule:** written client approval before naming a client, showing their assets, or publishing results.

### 4. Seminar Diaries

Real room footage, one participant problem, one live demo, one takeaway, one invitation to the next session.

**Growth job:** workshop inquiries and authority.

### 5. Pamangkin Challenge

Audience submits a safe, non-confidential task. Jeff tests one submission in the next episode.

**Growth job:** comments, repeat viewing, and community.

### 6. AI Tambayan LIVE

A weekly 30–40 minute live show. One useful build, one audience challenge, one soft service invitation.

**Growth job:** watch time, trust, leads, and reusable clips.

## Avatar decision

### Recommendation: Jeff + sidekick, not avatar-only

| Option | Reach | Trust | Service conversion | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Jeff on camera | High | **Highest** | **Highest** | Core format |
| Full AI avatar | Novel at first | Low–medium | Low | Do not use as main host |
| Jeff + disclosed Tito Bot | **High** | High | High | Test as supporting device |
| Faceless screen recording | Medium | Medium | Medium | Use only for demos |

The local AI-muse example shows that avatar engagement depends more on a consistent character bible and conversational voice than on visual polish. Tito AI already has the stronger asset: a real Filipino practitioner and trainer. Use a sidekick only where it increases entertainment.

### Tito Bot test

- Existing Tito AI robot icon becomes the sidekick.
- Maximum 2–4 seconds per appearance.
- Roles: wrong-answer buzzer, “Teka lang” safety warning, score reveal, prompt judge.
- Always disclosed as an AI/animated character.
- Test for four videos before expanding.

## Viral testing system

### Every post must pass five gates

1. **Stop:** understandable with sound off in one second.
2. **Stay:** tension, timer, choice, surprise, or story question by second three.
3. **Payoff:** visible result before extended explanation.
4. **Proof:** real screen, real footage, approved client evidence, or explicit demonstration.
5. **Action:** exactly one CTA matched to the post's job.

### Metrics by objective

- Reach: 3-second hold, average watch time, completion, shares.
- Authority: saves, profile visits, repeat viewers.
- Community: meaningful comments and live return viewers.
- Business: qualified `TRAINING`/`SYSTEM` inquiries and booked calls.

Views without follows, saves, comments, or inquiries are awareness—not complete growth.
