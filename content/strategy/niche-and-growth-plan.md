## The one-liner

> **"The warm, relatable Tito who teaches everyday Filipinos to use AI for free — before it replaces their job."**

This is not a tech channel. It is for: freelancers (1.5M+), guro, BPO workers afraid of being replaced, nanays/tatays, small business owners.

**Fear + aspiration = share-worthy content.**
Every video either removes a fear ("hindi mahirap 'to") or delivers a visible win ("ganito ang resulta").

## Content pillars

### Pillar 1 — Tool Demo (Mon / Wed)
Highest save rate. Core educational content.
- "Paano gamitin ang Claude para sa negosyo mo — libre"
- "5 libreng AI tools para sa Filipino freelancers"
- "Gemini para sa Gmail — ganito gamitin"

### Pillar 2 — Before / After (Sat)
Emotional, shareable, high retention.
- "3 oras ng trabaho → 5 minuto gamit ang AI"
- "Ginawa ko ang buong proposal sa 2 minuto — ganito"

### Pillar 3 — Fear → Reassurance (evergreen)
High anxiety audience = high engagement.
- "Hindi ka papalitan ng AI. Papalitan ka ng taong gumagamit ng AI."
- "Kung BPO worker ka — ito ang kailangan mong malaman ngayon"

### Pillar 4 — Everyday Filipino Life (Fri story format)
Warmth + identity. Builds community.
- "Ang trabaho ko, napalitan na ba?" ← **W24 Friday post**
- "Nanay + AI — ganito namin ginagamit sa bahay"
- "Sari-sari store + Claude — totoo ba?"

## Hook formula

Every video must open in second 1 with one of:
1. **Fear hook** — "Kung BPO worker ka, pakinggan mo 'to."
2. **Result hook** — "30 seconds lang. Tingnan mo 'to." (then show immediately)
3. **Identity hook** — "May negosyo ka at wala kang marketing budget?"
4. **Curiosity hook** — "Ito ang tanong na hindi mo pa naitatanong sa AI."

**Never open with "Kumusta" first** — hook lands in second 1, opener comes after.

## Boost strategy

**Rule:** Only boost posts that already have strong organic saves or shares — proven signal = lower CPM.

**Process:**
1. Post organically
2. Wait 24–48 hours
3. If 50+ saves OR 200+ shares → boost it

**Budget tiers:**
| Stage | Budget | When |
|-------|--------|------|
| Test | Php 500–1,000/day | First boost — prove the format |
| Scale | Php 2,000–3,000/day | Format proven, ROAS positive |

**Targeting:**
- Location: Philippines
- Age: 25–40
- Interests: technology, online business, productivity, freelancing, entrepreneurship

**Best boost candidate (June 2026):** "5 Libreng AI Tools Para Sa Inyong Negosyo" — 60–90s screen record + Taglish voiceover. Highest shareability + lowest CPM format.

**Platform for first boost:** Facebook (Page required — convert profiles first). TikTok Promote as secondary once organic is performing.

## Engagement tactics

1. **End every video with a YES/NO or CHOICE question** — "Claude o Gemini — alin ang subukan mo muna?"
2. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — TikTok/IG reward fast early engagement
3. **Pin first comment within 5 min** — bonus tip or tool link
4. **Post times (PHT):** TikTok = 8–10 PM. IG = 7–9 PM.
5. **Share to Facebook Stories immediately** after posting Reels
