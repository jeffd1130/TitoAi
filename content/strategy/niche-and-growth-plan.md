## The niche — one sentence

> **"The warm, relatable Tito who teaches everyday Filipinos to use AI for free — before it replaces their job."**

This is not a tech channel. It is not for developers. It is for:
- OFWs who need to translate, write letters, and communicate faster
- Freelancers (1.5M+) who need to do more work in less time
- BPO workers afraid of being replaced — who need to become the person who *uses* the AI
- Nanays and tatays managing households and side hustles
- Small business owners (sari-sari, online selling, food stalls)

**Fear + aspiration = share-worthy content.**
Every video should either remove a fear ("hindi mahirap 'to") or deliver a visible win ("ganito ang resulta").

## Hook formula that works for this audience

Every video must open with ONE of these:
1. **Identity hook** — "48 years old ako. Dati housedad." (people stay to see themselves)
2. **Fear hook** — "Kung BPO worker ka, pakinggan mo 'to."
3. **Curiosity hook** — "Ito ang tanong na hindi mo pa naitatanong sa AI."
4. **Result hook** — "30 seconds lang. Tingnan mo 'to." (then show immediately)

**Never open with "Kumusta" first** — hook must land in second 1.

---

## Engagement tactics (what drives algorithm)

1. **End every video with a YES/NO or CHOICE question** — not "comment below" but "Claude o Gemini — alin ang subukan mo muna?"
2. **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — TikTok/IG reward fast engagement
3. **Pin the first comment** — use it as a link or bonus tip
4. **Post within the window:** TikTok PH peak = 8–10 PM PHT. IG = 7–9 PM PHT. (Already aligned with schedule)
5. **Share to Facebook Stories immediately** after posting Reels

## Competitive landscape

**No strong Taglish AI educator exists on TikTok/Reels as of May 2026.**

Closest competitors:
- Generic English-language AI tutorial accounts (not relatable to Filipinos)
- "Lina Lately" (AI influencer — not educational)
- General Filipino tech channels (gadget-focused, not free-tools focused)

**Tito AI's moat:**
- Taglish = barrier to entry for foreign creators
- "Libre lahat" = removes the #1 objection (cost)
- "Everyday Filipino" framing = specific audience identity
- First mover = algorithm will surface Tito AI when Filipinos search "AI tutorial Tagalog"
