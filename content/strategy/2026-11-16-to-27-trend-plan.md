## Format evidence

- **Tito AI's own data** (last verified Aug 31 and Sep 14): named-persona, proof-first free-tool demos beat prompt-recitation and career-framed posts; pin allocation was inconsistent. **There is no current baseline.** The newest file in `reports/` (Sep 21) is a plan, not a metrics pull. Jeff should pull fresh TikTok/IG numbers before Nov 16 and set thresholds himself.
- **Carousels vs. video (external):** vendor blogs report carousels get more saves and comments on educational content; one independent study (Fanpage Karma, as cited by a vendor) found carousels get fewer shares than video. These are secondary and directional. TikTok's own "1.9x likes / 2.6x shares" figure is repeated by vendors, but I found **no primary 2026 TikTok study.** https://ttcalculator.net/learn/tiktok-photo-carousel-engagement/
- **Instagram:** Mosseri has named watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach as the top signals (Jan 2025, via secondary sources). The popular "sends are worth 3–5x likes" figure is **unsourced.**
- **Filipino AI educators:** I could not find verified performance data. Searches returned directory pages and non-AI education creators. I cannot say what formats other Filipino AI educators are winning with. Treat the lineup as hypotheses to test, not as copying a proven pattern.
