# W26 Monday — Brief
**Slot:** 01-mon-ai-tip
**Topic:** Ang Sabi Nila: "Pang-Matalino Lang Iyan"
**Format:** AI Tip Reel · Talking head · 45–60s
**Drop:** Monday June 22, 2026 · 8:00 PM PHT

## Concept
"Ang Sabi Nila..." myth-busting format. Open with the exact fear/excuse Filipinos actually say about AI — then flip it in 45 seconds with one concrete proof.

Target myth: **"Pang-matalino lang iyan. Hindi para sa akin."**

## Structure
- Hook (0–5s): Quote the myth directly, deadpan
- The flip (5–30s): Show someone unexpected using it — sari-sari owner, driver, lola, construction worker — real task, real result
- The point (30–45s): "Hindi pang-matalino. Pang-lahat."
- CTA: Ask viewers to comment which myth THEY'VE heard about AI

## Why this works
- High comment bait — people tag the friend who says this
- Pattern interrupt: opening with the myth sounds like you're agreeing, then pivot
- Applies to the new teachers audience too ("Hindi para sa mga guro 'yan")

## Target personas hit
Sari-sari owner · BPO worker · Guro/teacher · Nanay/tatay · Anyone who's heard "pang-matalino lang"

## Notes
- No B-roll needed — pure face cam
- Say the myth in a mocking-but-warm voice (not mean)
- The "proof" character should NOT be a tech person
