# Tito AI chibi host — first asset set

**Created:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** reusable visual pilot; not a published post or speaking-avatar video.

Jeff asked for a chibi Tito AI avatar to stand in for him on education posts and videos. The character is a stylized human illustration based on Jeff's solo classroom photo and the existing Tito AI color system. It is not a photograph, live seminar participant, or claim of a real on-camera performance.

## Files

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `docs/assets/avatars/tito-ai-chibi-wave.png` | Greeting, cover, intro/outro |
| `docs/assets/avatars/tito-ai-chibi-teach.png` | Prompt/output walkthrough, pointing into a screen area |
| `docs/assets/avatars/tito-ai-chibi-surprised.png` | AI mistake, myth-busting, reaction hook |
| `docs/renders/tito-ai-chibi-motion-preview.mp4` | Three-second silent 1080×1920 layout/motion test, not a finished Reel |

The PNGs are 1024×1536 with transparent backgrounds. Preserve the alpha channel when uploading to Canva or compositing video. The existing Tito AI logo remains the brand mark; the chibi is the presenter character, not a replacement logo.

## Usage rules

- Use the chibi as the main visual presenter in upcoming **small-business AI lessons**; put a real prompt, real tool output, or clearly labeled practice example beside it. A cute avatar is a hook, not proof.
- In a 5-slide carousel, use the **surprised** pose on the problem/mistake slide, **teach** on the demo slide, and **wave** at the close. Leave enough space for legible content; avoid repeating the full character on every slide.
- In 9:16 videos, show the avatar as a cutout over screen recordings or simple backgrounds. The included motion preview has a gentle vertical bounce and no speech or audio. Do not imply lip-sync or Jeff's voice is present until an actual voice/video pass is produced.
- Keep on-screen claims grounded. Never imply the illustrated character attended a seminar or served a client. Use real seminar footage only with event details and participant permissions verified.
- Use a brighter pilot palette for new content: warm cream `#FFF8E7`, marigold `#FFB800`, coral `#FF6B4A`, deep teal `#0D6B63`, and navy text `#0A0F1E`. Existing logo and archive assets stay unchanged. Avoid small white text on coral.
- Keep the avatar recognizably consistent: short black hair, subtle chin stubble, white shirt, blue jeans, white shoes, friendly adult chibi proportions, orange/teal edge accents. Do not present it as a real photo or undisclosed human testimony.

## Generation record

Built-in imagegen mode, `stylized-concept`: reference Jeff's solo classroom portrait for likeness and the existing Tito AI logo for colors/mood; create a polished adult Filipino human chibi with transparent background, then two consistent pose/expression variants. No text, no invented people or event details. The pose files were saved into the project; discarded variations are not part of the asset set.
