# Tito AI — Why the origin videos are getting low views
**Date:** Mon Nov 9, 2026 (PHT) · **Prior report:** `2026-11-05-weekly-checkbalance.md`
**Sources (live pull, Nov 9):** TikTok Studio (per-post analytics) and Meta Business Suite content insights for **Tito Ai PH**. Raw numbers: `reports/data/2026-11-09-metrics.json`. Meta per-post numbers are IG + FB combined (cross-posted).

## 1. Top line

- **Main cause: people swipe in the first second.** TikTok says most viewers of Parts 1 and 2 left at **0:01**. Average watch is **3.5–3.7 s of 30 s**, and only **1–3%** watch to the end. On IG/FB the average play is **2–3 s**, and only **14–15%** stay past 3 seconds.
- **So the apps stop showing it.** TikTok shows a new video to about 100–200 strangers first (91.6% of Part 1's views came from For You). With 1–3% completion, it isn't pushed further, which is why Part 1 stopped at **119** and Part 2 at **85**. Your best older posts held people twice as long (6.5–6.8 s average, 11–13% completion).
- **Instagram's "200+" is IG + Facebook together:** Part 1 has 284 and Part 2 has 287, about 208 viewers each, so IG alone is lower. IG reached only **79 accounts in 28 days**, so Reels depend almost entirely on strangers, the same first-second test as TikTok.

## 3. Why the first second loses people

1. **The opening frame looks like a title card.** The logo, a "PART 1 / 5" badge and a big text block show before anything happens. Strangers see a series label for a story they haven't started and swipe.
2. **"Part 2" shuts out new viewers.** Over 90% of views come from strangers (For You on TikTok; 94% non-followers on FB). "PART 2 / 5" plus "So paano ko sinimulan?" tells them they missed something.
3. **No payoff for the viewer in second 0–2.** The best posts promised something useful right away ("5 minuto lang — resume + cover letter"). The origin story is about Jeff, and the reason to keep watching ("what's in it for me") comes late or not at all.
4. **Animated character, voice-over, slow first motion.** The chibi barely moves in the first second, and there's no real face or real screen. Your top posts used real footage or a real result.
5. **Posting time drifted.** Part 1 went up at 12:09 PM and Part 2 at 4:19 PM, not the 7 PM slot. This is minor next to points 1–4.

## 4. Boost call

**No.** With 2–3 s average watch and 1–3% completion, paid views would hit the same first-second drop. Fix the opening first, then reconsider.

## 5. What to change (no spend)

- **Parts 3–5 (not yet live):** start the voice on frame 1 with the strongest line. Make the "PART n / 5" badge small or bring it in after 2 s, and replace the big text card with a one-line viewer payoff (for example, "Walang background? Ito ang ginawa ko"). Show motion in the first second.
- **Make each part stand alone:** open with one sentence of context instead of assuming viewers saw the last part. Put "Part 3 of 5" in the caption, not the first frame.
- **Pin the Clark restaurant story again** (4,747 views; it's no longer pinned). Profile visitors currently see three low-performing pins.
- **Post at the planned slot** and test one morning post (about 7 AM PHT), when followers were most active.
- **Next pull:** 72 hours after Part 3. Success = TikTok average watch above 6 s and IG/FB 3-second views above 25%.
