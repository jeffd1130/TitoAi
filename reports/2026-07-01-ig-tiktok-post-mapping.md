## Key patterns worth cross-referencing against the content calendar

1. **Pinning, not format, drives the biggest view swings on TikTok.** All 3 pinned posts (784, 761, 630 views) are also the top 3 posts overall — each is a script that was posted twice, and the pinned copy outperforms its unpinned duplicate by roughly 2-4x every time.
2. **Photo/carousel posts on TikTok do not underperform video** — they land in the same 207-272 view range as most videos. One script ("Ang trabaho mo, safe pa ba?") was posted as both photo (247 views) and video (224 views), with the photo edging out the video.
3. **On Instagram, slide-style Reels and talking-head Reels score similarly** (~190-260 avg views each) — no consistent format advantage either way. Variance tracks more with topic specificity (e.g., "Mga Guro" lesson-plan post at 297-304 views) and whether the caption asks a direct question.
4. **Duplicate-script posting is a deliberate/recurring pattern on both platforms** — 5 scripts were posted twice each (3 on TikTok, corresponding pairs on IG), likely testing hook/audio variants.
