## Gaps & Needs for Improvement

### 1. FYP views NOT converting to followers
- 4,500 views → only 33 profile visits → unknown follower adds
- 93.7% FYP = algorithm loves it, but FYP audience doesn't intend to follow
- No consistent follow CTA inside the carousel (currently only in caption)

### 2. Almost zero comments (2 this week)
- Seed comment exists but replies in first hour are inconsistent
- No comment-starter in slides themselves
- Algorithm rewards comment threads — currently missing this signal

### 3. Search traffic at 1% only
- Guro search queries are clearly present but only driving 1% of views
- No SEO-optimized caption structure (hashtags only, no keyword-rich first line)

### 4. No pinned series or playlist on TikTok
- "TitoSerye" is appearing organically in search
- No official TikTok Series/Playlist created yet — viewers can't self-sort into the story content

### 5. Split boost budget diluting impact
- W29 boost was split ₱300 + ₱200 across 2 posts = ~₱250/day avg
- Carousels can't be boosted via in-app Promote — forces Ads Manager for next round
