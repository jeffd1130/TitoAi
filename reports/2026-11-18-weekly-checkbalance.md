## 3. Why followers aren't growing
1. **Wrong promise for strangers.** A personal story ("my journey") asks strangers to care about Jeff before they get any value. The resume post promises a result in the first second, and it got 10× the views.
2. **No reason to follow.** Every post is a one-off. There's no repeatable series name with a "next episode" promise tied to a job, money or school problem.
3. **Low engagement signals.** 4 likes and 0 shares a week tells TikTok to stop pushing. Posts don't ask for one easy action that people actually take (save, or "comment RESUME").
4. **Facebook is a ghost page.** 28 followers and no groups. Class B/C Filipinos discover things in **Facebook groups** (VA, teachers, BPO, online sellers), not on new Pages.
5. **Search is underused.** 26.6% of TikTok views come from search, but titles aren't written as searches ("paano gumawa ng resume gamit AI").
