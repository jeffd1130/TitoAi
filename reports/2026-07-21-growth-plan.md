## Why Slides Are Winning

### Algorithm reasons
| What happens | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Each swipe = interaction event | More engagement signals per impression than a reel |
| Carousel forces dwell time | TikTok rewards longer session time before next scroll |
| Text in slides gets indexed | Search traffic finds carousels directly ("Gemini lesson plan" → our slide) |
| Reference content gets saved | Saves are the highest-weight signal on both TikTok and IG |

## Why Reels Are Underperforming

1. **Hook gap** — talking head opener ("Kumusta") loses the algo in second 1. Reels need a visual action or bold verbal hook before word one.
2. **No retention anchor** — carousels force commitment (you chose to swipe). Reels let viewers scroll past passively.
3. **Wrong content type** — step-by-step AI demos don't translate well to 60-90s talking head. Too much to absorb in one watch.
4. **Reels work for emotion** — Friday stories (S1E1, S1E2) are better suited to reels because they're narrative, not instructional.
