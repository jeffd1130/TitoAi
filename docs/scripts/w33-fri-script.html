EPISODE CONTEXT
True story: July 6, 2026 — Jeff's first corporate/school AI training at CJEF. ~12 participants: guro, admin, school directors. Tools taught: Claude + Gemini (free). Full day. Certificate ceremony at the end.

Why this works for S2E2: S2E1 established Jeff as a PM who uses AI for clients. S2E2 shows the next layer — he now teaches it to others. This builds credibility for the 2nd batch CTA and shows that the training is real and has already happened. "Ika-2 batch" becomes believable because Batch 1 is proven.
RECORDING SETUP
Format: Talking head. Direct to camera. Warm, reflective energy — you're sharing a memory that moved you.

Tone: Honest and a little surprised — like you didn't expect how it would feel to teach. Not proud, just genuinely moved.

Key moment: The participant who said "kaya pala" — that's the emotional core. Pause there. Let it breathe.

Hook rule: Open with the scene, not a greeting. Drop the audience into the room immediately.
Script
01Hook — Into the Room0:00–0:12
Noong July 6, nandoon ako sa harap ng sampung guro at school director. Hindi ako student. Ako ang nagtuturo. At ang tinuturo ko? Claude at Gemini — parehong libre. [no greeting. open mid-scene. "Hindi ako student. Ako ang nagtuturo." — pause between the two lines. contrast lands harder with silence.]
02The Story — What Happened0:12–0:42
Isang buong araw ang training. Guro, admin, school directors — mga taong bihasa sa kanilang trabaho, pero hindi pa natutukan ang AI. Doon namin buksan ang claude.ai para sa unang beses sa kanila. Sinabi ng isa sa kanila — pagkatapos na makita ang output ni Claude: "Kaya pala." Dalawang salita lang. Pero alam mo kung ano ang ibig sabihin noon. Naiintindihan na niya kung bakit may advantage ang mga taong gumagamit nito sa mga hindi pa. [slow down at "Kaya pala." Two words — let them land. Don't rush to explain. The pause IS the point.]
03The Point — Anyone Can Learn0:42–1:00
Hindi kailangan ng coding. Hindi kailangan ng tech background. Kailangan lang ng willingness na subukan — at ng tamang gabay. Iyan ang natutunan nila sa isang araw. Iyan ang maaari ring matutunan mo. [confident, calm. "Iyan ang maaari ring matutunan mo" — look directly into the camera here. this line is the bridge to the CTA.]
04Closer + 2nd Batch CTA1:00–1:20
Nagsisimula na kami ng ika-2 batch ng aming AI class. Gusto mong sumali sa susunod — bilang kumpanya o bilang indibidwal? Mag-comment o mag-DM sa amin. Ingat lagi, mga Pamangkin. Tito AI — AI Para Sa Ating Lahat. [warm, unhurried close. "bilang kumpanya o bilang indibidwal" — slight pause between the two. let them self-identify. not a pitch — an open door.]
