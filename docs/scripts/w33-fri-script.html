EPISODE CONTEXT
True story: July 6, 2026 — Jeff's first corporate/school AI training at CJEF. Approximately 12 participants: guro, admin, and school leaders. Tools taught: Claude + Gemini (free). Full day. Certificate ceremony at the end.

Why this works for S2E2: S2E1 established Jeff as a PM who uses AI for clients. S2E2 shows the next layer — he now teaches it to others. This builds credibility for the 2nd batch CTA and shows that the training is real and has already happened. "Ika-2 batch" becomes believable because Batch 1 is proven.

Format: Direct-to-camera hook and closer, with real CJEF room, teaching, laptop, and certificate footage through the middle. No text overlays.

Tone: Honest and a little surprised — like you didn't expect how it would feel to teach. Not proud, just genuinely moved.

Proof: Use real CJEF room, teaching, laptop, participant, and certificate footage. Do not use an unverified direct quote.

Hook rule: Open with the result and real scene. Add “Kumusta, mga Pamangkin!” only after the hook.
