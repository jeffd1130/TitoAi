SEASON 2 ARC
Season 1 was Jeff's real client stories — Cobrinha, Pares, the website build, the juggling act.

Season 2 opens with the bigger picture: AI as the multiplier for any role, any industry. Jeff reintroduces himself to new followers as a Social Media Project Manager handling different clients across different industries — and shows that AI is the system behind it. Theme: "Iba't ibang kliyente. Iisang AI. Kaya ng lahat."
RECORDING SETUP
Format: Talking head. Direct to camera. No screen share needed — personal story, not a demo.

Energy: Confident from the start. You know what you're doing — the story is about scale, not survival.

Hook rule: First 3 seconds = hook before "Kumusta." Open with the scale contrast — deliver clean, direct eye contact.

Training CTA: Lands at the end, after the closer. Keep it conversational — "bilang kumpanya o bilang indibidwal" gives them a clear choice without pressure.
Script
01Hook — The Scale0:00–0:10
Social Media Project Manager ako. Iba't ibang kliyente. Iba't ibang industriya. Iisang tao — ako. Taga-Manila. Paano? AI. [no greeting. straight to the scale. pause after "ako" — let the solo-operator reality land before the answer.]
02The Setup — What AI Actually Does0:10–0:35
Hindi papalit ng AI ang trabaho mo. Pero pinalaki nito ang kaya ko — content, captions, strategy, reports — para sa iba't ibang kliyente, sa parehong oras na mayroon ako. Dalawa lang ang tools ko: Claude at Gemini — libre ang dalawa. Hindi ito para sa mga techie lang. Hindi ito para sa mga malaking kumpanya lang. Para ito sa sinuman na may trabaho at gustong gumawa ng mas marami nang hindi nagpapalago ng problema. [measured pace here. "pinalaki nito ang kaya ko" is the key line — repeat it with your eyes if needed. the "libre ang dalawa" is always worth pausing on.]
03The Bridge — Any Role, Any Industry0:35–0:55
Guro ka? Freelancer? VA? BPO? May sariling negosyo? Ang AI — hindi nito kailangan na maging eksperto ka. Kailangan lang nito na ikaw ay marunong sa trabaho mo. At doon ka na eksperto. [shorter sentences here. direct. let each line breathe. "doon ka na eksperto" is the reframe — own the delivery.]
04Closer + Training CTA0:55–1:20
Iyan ang ibinabahagi ko rito — practical tips, libre tools, para sa lahat ng Pilipino. Ito na ang Season 2. Mas marami pa. Mas praktikal pa. At kung gusto mong matuto nang mas malalim — nagsisimula na kami ng ika-2 batch ng aming AI class. Gusto mong sumali sa susunod na batch — bilang kumpanya o bilang indibidwal? Mag-comment o mag-DM sa amin. Ingat lagi, mga Pamangkin. Tito AI — AI Para Sa Ating Lahat. [warm but confident close. "bilang kumpanya o bilang indibidwal" — pause between the two options so viewers can self-select mentally. not an ad — just an open door.]
