RECORDING SETUP
Format: Talking head. Face cam only. Same setup as S1E1 and S1E2.

Tone: Honest and warm. Not bragging — just showing the reality. The audience should feel "oo, ganyan din ako." The AI is the equalizer, not the hero.

Key beat: Pause after "apat na kliyente" — let it land before you continue. That number is the hook.

No fabrication: Stick to what's real. Don't mention specific client names, follower counts, or revenue figures. "Maraming kliyente" and "naibigay ko ang trabaho" is enough.

May mga araw na — graphic designer ako ng umaga. data analyst ng tanghali. at project manager ng gabi. Iisang araw. Tatlong papel. Apat na kliyente. [direct cam. calm, not overwhelmed. "apat na kliyente" — pause here. let it land.]

Yun ang pinaka-importanteng bagay para sa akin — hindi ang maging busy. Kundi ang maging present. [warm shift. "maging present" is the emotional core — this is what resonates with Mga Pamangkin who are also balancing work and family.]
