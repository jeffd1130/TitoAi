The Niche

"The warm, relatable Tito who teaches everyday Filipinos to use AI for free — before it replaces their job."
Not a tech channel. Not for developers. For:
  • OFWs — translate docs, write formal letters, communicate faster
  • Freelancers (1.5M+) — do more work in less time, stay competitive
  • BPO workers — become the person who uses AI before it replaces them
  • Nanays + tatays — managing households and side hustles
  • Small business owners — sari-sari, online selling, food stalls
Fear + aspiration = share-worthy. Every video removes a fear ("hindi mahirap ito") or delivers a visible win ("ganito ang resulta").

Hook Formula

  • Identity hook "48 years old ako. Dati housedad."
    People stay to see themselves in the story.
  • Fear hook "Kung BPO worker ka, pakinggan mo 'to."
    High anxiety audience — high engagement.
  • Curiosity hook "Ito ang tanong na hindi mo pa naitatanong sa AI."
    Creates a gap they need to fill.
  • Result hook "30 seconds lang. Tingnan mo 'to."
    Then show immediately — no delay.
Never open with "Kumusta" first — hook must land in second 1.
