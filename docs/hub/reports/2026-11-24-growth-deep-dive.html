Reading it:

  1. Hook leak: 90%+ of viewers leave in the first 3 seconds, so the algorithm stops showing the post.
  2. Profile leak: fewer than 1 in 100 viewers opens the profile. Nothing in the video gives a reason to see more ("Part 2 is on my page", "10 more prompts in my profile").
  3. Follow leak: people who visit the profile don't follow. The profile has no clear promise or series, and the pins are old Origin parts.
  4. Lead leak: there is no link to click. The PROMPT PDF is DM-only, and the waitlist Form doesn't exist yet.

3. Audience and timing

  • Who follows us on TikTok:
  • 87% male, 13% female.
  • Only 62.7% are in the Philippines.
  • Ages 25–34: 33.6%; ages 18–24: 27.2%.
  • What this means: a big part of our 1K followers is not our Class B/C Filipino buyer. Many likely came from the July Pares story boost, which had broad reach. Teachers, VAs and BPO workers skew more female and more Filipino, so persona posts (guro, VA, CSR) should fix the mix over time.
  • Where views come from (TikTok): For You 90%, search 9% (it was 27% the week before).
  • Search is a free, steady source.
  • Captions that start with the exact search phrase ("paano gumawa ng resume gamit AI") will catch more of it.
  • When followers are active: 6 AM peak (TikTok). We post at 7–8 PM, and recent posts drifted from 6:36 to 8:42 PM.

4. Diagnosis: 6 reasons views and followers are stuck

  1. The first second doesn't show the payoff.
  • Evidence: 2–4 s average watch, 5–10% past 3 s.
  • Openings show a title card or the chibi saying hello before the result.
  1. Too many story and series parts; too few practical posts.
  • Evidence: Origin parts got 90–99 TikTok views. Practical posts got 500–1,100.
  • A stranger has no reason to care about "Part 4".
  1. No reason to follow.
  • Evidence: 0 follows from every November reel.
  • Posts don't say what you get by following ("bawat Lunes, Miyerkules, Biyernes: isang libreng AI para sa trabaho mo").
  1. Too few posts.
  • Evidence: 3 a week versus the planned 5. The Tue/Thu/Sat extras are all still drafts.
  • Fewer posts mean fewer chances to hit.
  1. Weak engagement signals.
  • Evidence: 0 saves, 2 shares, 1 comment per week on TikTok.
  • PROMPT comments are now starting (3–4 per post). That is the right lever, but replies and DMs have to happen in the first hour.
  1. Paid push stopped.
  • Evidence: the July ₱200 story boost gave +365 followers. There have been no boosts since August.
  • Organic alone hasn't moved followers in 3 months.
