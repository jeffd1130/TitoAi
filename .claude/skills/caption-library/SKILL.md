# caption-library

Rewrite the captions and hashtags for one slot without touching the design.

## Trigger phrases
"redo the caption for Monday", "regenerate hashtags", "rewrite Wednesday's caption"

## Steps

1. **Identify the slot** and read its `brief.md`, `drafts/script.md` and current `drafts/captions.md` so the new version doesn't repeat itself.
2. **Write new captions (Taglish)**, separate sections **TikTok**, **Instagram**, **Facebook**, plus a **Seed comment**:
   - Lead with the hook or the win; 3–5 sentences; one CTA; max 3 emoji.
   - Openers/closers may come from `brand/caption-pool.json`.
   - Five hashtags per platform, always including `#TitoAIPH #MgaPamangkin #AIParaSaAtin`.
   - Fit the audience (Class B/C first, some A); no urgency tactics or hard sell.
3. **Show Jeff the new captions** before saving.
4. **On his OK**, overwrite `drafts/captions.md`. The Studio's Approve & Post card picks it up automatically.
