## 1. What Tito AI is

**Channel:** @TitoAIPH (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)
**Creator:** Jeff de las Armas — Senior Marketing Analyst, Manila, PH (based in San Jose, CA originally; content is Manila-time PHT)
**Concept:** A Filipino AI education channel. "Tito AI" is a warm, relatable uncle character who teaches everyday Filipinos how to use free AI tools (Claude, Gemini) — no jargon, all Taglish.

**One-liner / niche positioning:**
> "The warm, relatable Tito who teaches everyday Filipinos to use AI for free — before it replaces their job."

This is explicitly **not** a tech-bro/tools-roundup channel. It's a trust-and-warmth play aimed at people who are anxious about AI, not excited about it.

**Target audience ("Mga Pamangkin" — the nephews/nieces):**
- Freelancers (1.5M+ in PH)
- Guro/teachers (900K+ in public school alone — highest-trust community multiplier: one teacher reaches ~40 families)
- BPO workers (1.9M, anxious about AI replacing their jobs)
- Nanays/tatays (parents)
- Small business owners / negosyante

**Market rationale (why this niche, why now):**
- 12.7M Filipino workers exposed to GenAI disruption — highest in ASEAN (ILO 2026)
- Only 14.9% of small PH firms use AI tools (Philippine AI Report 2025)
- 42.4% of PH internet users use ChatGPT monthly — 6th globally (Radar PH 2026)
- 83% of Filipino students already use AI; parents haven't caught up (BusinessWorld 2026)
- Gap: millions exposed to AI disruption, almost no relatable Taglish-speaking guide exists. First-mover in this lane.

**Competitive moat:** Taglish (foreign creators can't replicate), "libre lahat" (removes the #1 objection — cost), warm tito brand energy no one else owns in PH, first-mover SEO on "AI tutorial Tagalog" searches.

**Avoid:** generic "Top 5 AI tools" listicles, ChatGPT tips with no Filipino angle, "AI will take your job" fear-only content with no solution offered.

## 2. Voice & brand

**Tone:** warm older kuya/tito — never lecturing, never selling. Calm confidence, "hindi parang nagbebenta."
**Language:** Taglish, natural mix, never forced. No pure English unless quoting a tool/UI.
**Standard opener:** "Kumusta, mga Pamangkin!" (but see hook rule below — this comes *after* the hook, not as the video's first line)
**Standard closer:** "Ingat lagi, mga Pamangkin. Tito AI — AI Para Sa Ating Lahat."
**Length:** 60–90 seconds max for TikTok/Reels — never longer.
**Rule #1 — no fabrication:** never invent quotes, comments, metrics, follower reactions, or events. Real tools, real prompts, real output only.
**Free tools only:** Claude (claude.ai) and Gemini (gemini.google.com) are the only tools ever featured or recommended. Never paid tools.

**Visual identity:**
- Dark navy background (`#0A0F1E`) dominant
- Gold accent (`#F59E0B`) — primary highlight
- Teal accent (`#0D9488`) — secondary
- Bebas Neue (headlines) + DM Sans (body/captions)
- Never cartoonish, never overly tech-looking — warm, human, editorial

**Caption style:** short, punchy, Taglish, lead with hook/win, max 3–5 sentences, one CTA max, max 3 emoji. Hashtags 8–15, always include `#TitoAIPH #MgaPamangkin #AIParaSaAtin`. Captions are written per-platform (TikTok short/8 tags, Instagram medium/15 tags, Facebook story-length/6 tags) — never one caption reused across all three.

**Hook rule (critical):** the hook must land in second 1. Never open with "Kumusta" first. Use one of:
1. Fear hook — "Kung BPO worker ka, pakinggan mo 'to."
2. Result hook — "30 seconds lang. Tingnan mo 'to." (show immediately)
3. Identity hook — "May negosyo ka at wala kang marketing budget?"
4. Curiosity hook — "Ito ang tanong na hindi mo pa naitatanong sa AI."

**Algorithm tactics:** end every video with a YES/NO or choice question ("Claude o Gemini — alin ang subukan mo muna?"). Reply to every comment in the first hour. Pin the first comment within 5 minutes of posting.

## 4. What's proven to work (data from the channel's own run, as of Aug 3, 2026)

- **Named-persona, step-by-step, free-tool demo carousels are the top-proven format** — won 3 consecutive weeks (W27 Wed → W28 Wed → W29 Wed), including the channel's best post ever (1,016 views/63 likes in 2 days).
- **Boosted story reels are the strongest follower-conversion lever found to date** — a ₱200 boost on a Friday story reel drove 365 new followers vs. a ₱300 carousel boost driving only ~1,100 views and far fewer follows. This beat carousel-boost follower conversion by >100x.
- "Pin the post immediately, never duplicate-post" was an early standing rule, but by mid-July data started contradicting it (unpinned duplicates outperforming pinned originals in some cases) — **treat pin-vs-unpinned as unsettled**, not a hard rule.
- TikTok is the primary/discovery platform (algorithm-favored education niche, viral reach, near-zero required spend). Instagram is secondary (authority/depth, converts lurkers, 25–34 urban professionals). Facebook is support/paid-boost only (cheapest PH CPM, needs a converted Page — personal profiles can't run ads).
- As of Aug 3, 2026: TikTok crossed 1,012 followers; Instagram at 75 followers (growth much slower on IG so far).

## 5. Growth / boost strategy

**Boost budget:** originally Php 2,000/week for a 3-week ramp (W26–W28), rotating one new platform in each week (TikTok only → +Instagram → +Facebook). Current guidance based on proven data: **prioritize boosting Friday story reels for follower growth**, not Wednesday carousels (carousels win on views/saves but convert far fewer new followers per peso).

**Targeting (all boosts):** Philippines, age 22–45, language Filipino/Tagalog, interests: Technology, Online Business, Productivity, Freelancing, Education. Use automatic/smart targeting on TikTok and IG (outperforms manual on new accounts).

**KPIs tracked weekly:** new followers/week, total video views/week, watch completion rate, saves per post, cost per new follower, boost spend. Month-3 targets: 500+ new TikTok followers/week, 300+ IG+FB followers/week, 50,000+ total weekly views, >50% watch completion, 100+ saves/post, <Php 15 cost/follower.
